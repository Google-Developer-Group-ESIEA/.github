## `// what is a GDG?`

**GDG on Campus ESIEA** is a student club in the **Google Developer Groups** program, backed by **Google for Developers**. We learn, build, and connect around Google Cloud, AI & Gemini, and web/mobile development, all on **real projects**.

| Official program | Global network | No level required |
|---|---|---|
| Part of Google Developer Groups, backed by Google for Developers. | GDG campuses all around the world. | Dev or not, just the urge to build. |

---

## `// why join`

- **The Google badge:** an official program on your résumé and access to Google Cloud certifications.
- **Cloud & AI, hands-on:** Google Cloud, AI & Gemini, web and mobile dev workshops, all on real projects.
- **The right network:** Googlers, alumni mentors, founders, and the other GDG campuses.
- **A career boost:** in-demand skills to land internships & apprenticeships.

---

## `// our roadmap`

| Format | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Workshops** | Hands-on sessions inside our events: Google Cloud, AI & Gemini, web & mobile. Bring a laptop, leave having built something. |
| **Hackathon** | Our signature format. An inter-school & inter-GDG series. *Agentic AI in action: design, not prompting.* |
| **DevFest ESIEA** | Our annual conference: talks, workshops, demos, tech speakers. |
| **Career Connect** | A day dedicated to opportunities: internships, apprenticeships, recruiters, alumni. |
